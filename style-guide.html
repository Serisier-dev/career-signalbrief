Brand and product direction

RoleSignal style guide.

A concise product language for useful career briefs: calm, selective, specific, and transparent about uncertainty.

Name

RoleSignal
Short, practical, and focused on signal over search volume.

Positioning

Fewer better-fit roles, explained clearly. Not a job board. A career brief.

Tone

Plain English, direct recommendations, visible watch-outs, no inflated confidence.

Design tokens

Dark Linear-inspired UI: near-black canvas, subtle translucent cards, Inter typography, violet CTAs, and compact report cards. The visual goal is credible and calm, not flashy.

RoleSignal is a lightweight prototype by Mike's job-signal workflow. Static site, no tracking, no backend.
